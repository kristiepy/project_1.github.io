Our Dearest Conductor

Mark Saresky

Since 2008 Mark Saresky has been celebrated for his collaborative and engaging approach, expressive musicality, and diverse programming as Music Director and Principal Conductor of the Hart House Symphonic Band at the University of Toronto.
