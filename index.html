Our Mission

The Hart House Symphonic Band at the University of Toronto is a vibrant ensemble that brings together students, alumni, and community members from diverse academic backgrounds, united by their shared passion for music. Open to musicians of all skill levels, the band provides a supportive environment where players can develop their talents, collaborate, and perform a wide range of symphonic repertoire. With regular performances at Hart House and across the university, the band enriches the cultural life of the campus, offering an outlet for creativity and a sense of community through the universal language of music.
